First 5K Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application First 5K (hereafter referred to as "the Application") for mobile devices, which was created by Rick James. The Application is designed to provide fitness tracking for a running 5K entirely on your device.

Information Collection and Use

The Application requests access to your device's precise location to enable core features, specifically recording your run on a map.

Strictly Local Processing: Any and all location data accessed by the Application is processed dynamically and stored strictly within the isolated internal storage of your personal mobile device.

No Cloud Transmission: This data is never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

Future Health Tracking: If the Application introduces manual health metrics tracking or phone sensor analysis in future updates, all such physical or health-related data will similarly be processed and stored strictly local-on-device.

Third-Party Access and Data Sharing

Because your data remains localized on your device, we do not collect, monitor, or have any access to your information.

We do not share, sell, trade, or rent your location or personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

The Application does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs that harvest user behavior or background data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your location information is stored on your device for as long as you choose to keep it within the app's history.

You can clear your location history at any time using the in-app settings.

Uninstalling the Application will completely delete all locally stored data from your device automatically.

Children’s Privacy

The Application does not collect or transmit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since all data is processed strictly on-device, no data is accessible to the developer or any external entity.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our local data architecture or future integrations. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page and will be effective immediately upon publishing.
